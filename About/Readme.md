# About the name of project

## Jejune definition

- [Wiktionary](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/jejune)
  1. not nutritious
  2. **Lacking matter; empty; devoid of substance**
  3. Naive; simplistic
- [Collins dictionary](https://www.collinsdictionary.com/us/dictionary/english/jejune)
  1. without interest or significance; dull; insipid
  2. juvenile; immature; childish
  3. **lacking knowledge or experience**; uninformed
  4. deficient or lacking in nutritive value
- [Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/jejune)
  1. **Devoid of significance** or interest; dull
  2. juvenile, puerile
  3. lacking nutritive value

## Jejune quotes

- From [`Arthur Mervyn; or Memoirs of the Year 1793` by Charles Brockden Brown](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/18508/pg18508-images.html#:~:text=Books%20are%20cold%2C%20jejune%2C%20vexatious%20in%20their%20sparingness%20of%20information%20at%20one%20time%20and%20their%20impertinent%20loquacity%20at%20another.)

  ```text
  Books are cold, jejune, vexatious in their sparingness of information at one time and their impertinent loquacity at another.
  ```

- From [`The roll-call` by Arnold Bennett](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/12654/12654-h/12654-h.htm#:~:text=The%20reality%20did%20not%20correspond;%20it%20transcended%20his%20imagination;%20it%20painfully%20demonstrated%20his%20jejune%20crudity.)

  ```text
  The reality did not correspond; it transcended his imagination; it painfully demonstrated his jejune crudity.
  ```

- From [`Atlas Shrugged` by Ayn Rand](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/Atlas_Shrugged#About_Atlas_Shrugged)

  ```text
  Each time, I found myself oscillating between fits of the giggles, at the awful prose, and irritation, at the jejune philosophy. 
  ```

- From [`The Catholic World, Vol. V., No. 28 July, 1867.`](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/53935/53935-h/53935-h.htm#:~:text=its%20systems%20of%20dogmatic%20theology%20are%20extremely%20jejune%20and%20discordant.)
